## Brand & Style
The design system embodies a premium, cinematic luxury editorial style crafted for high-tier personal presentation. It projects quiet authority, architectural restraint, and bespoke craftsmanship.

### Visual Pillars
- **Cinematic Noir:** Infinite dark layers built from nuanced near-blacks (`#050606` and `#0B0C0C`) paired with atmospheric lighting, low-key portraiture, and high focal clarity.
- **Editorial Precision:** Typographic dominance through generous tracking, outlined transparent display typography, structured micro-labels, and sharp visual hierarchy reminiscent of luxury monographs.
- **Muted Gold Accents:** Sophisticated metallic yellow highlights (`#E6C84A`) used sparingly as surgical focal points—indicating active states, key interactive links, and elevated markers.
- **Linear Structuralism:** Hairline translucent white and gold dividers (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`) establish rigorous spatial divisions without visual clutter.

## Colors
The palette relies on a stark value contrast between near-black depth and crisp alabaster text, punctuated by warm champagne gold.

### Color Roles
- **Base Canvas (`#050606`):** The foundational dark-room surface providing maximum contrast and cinematic immersion.
- **Surface Elevation (`#0B0C0C`):** Secondary container surface for cards, navigation drawers, and floating panels.
- **Accent Gold (`#E6C84A`):** Reserved strictly for active indicators, primary call-to-actions, category tags, and selected micro-dots.
- **Primary Typographic Tone (`#F5F5F5`):** 96% luminance off-white for crisp, legible editorial reading without eye-strain glare.
- **Secondary Typographic Tone (`#999999`):** Neutral gray used for supporting blurbs, metadata, and structural outlines.
- **Structural Borders:** Hairline dividers rendered in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)` or subtly tinted `rgba(230, 200, 74, 0.25)` on hover.

## Typography
Typographic rhythm combines the geometric, industrial silhouette of **Space Grotesk** with the neutral legibility of **Plus Jakarta Sans** and **Inter**.

### Style Rules
- **Outlined Big Typography:** The signature hero title uses transparent fill with an outer stroke (`-webkit-text-stroke: 1.5px rgba(245, 245, 245, 0.85)` or gold `rgba(230, 200, 74, 0.7)`), paired with wide tracking.
- **Editorial Sub-headers:** Display levels are set in uppercase or tight title case, maintaining strict vertical baseline alignment.
- **Metadata and Micro-Labels:** Rendered exclusively in all-caps using **Inter** with heavy letter spacing (`0.2em`+) for navigation tags, dates, and category callouts.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout uses an asymmetrical, editorial canvas anchored by fixed side rails and cinematic widescreen framing.

### Structural Framework
- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** Fixed vertical utility spine (80px width) on the left margin housing persistent social links and rotation indicators. The content area leverages a 12-column grid with generous 3.5rem margins and 2rem gutters.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout with 2rem margins. Vertical navigation collapses into a consolidated top bar.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with 1.25rem margins. Outsized typography downscales dynamically to maintain single-viewport visual balance.
- **Vertical Spacing:** Section transitions rely on `space-xl` and higher multipliers, giving hero images and design case previews breathing room.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation is achieved through tone-on-tone surface separation, backdrop blurring, and atmospheric radial illumination rather than traditional dropshadows.

### Layering Model
- **Ground Floor:** `#050606` deep base canvas.
- **Atmospheric Layer:** Subtle radial gradients (`radial-gradient(circle at center, rgba(230, 200, 74, 0.04) 0%, transparent 70%)`) placed behind imagery to establish subtle spotlight depth.
- **Floating Overlays:** `#0B0C0C` panels with a `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` and crisp 1px borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`).
- **Interactive Depth:** Hovering over cards lifts borders to `rgba(230, 200, 74, 0.45)` with a faint ambient glow (`0 0 24px rgba(230, 200, 74, 0.08)`).

## Shapes
A razor-sharp, architectural aesthetic is enforced across all surfaces and components (`roundedness: 0`). 

Sharp 90-degree corners accentuate the editorial magazine format. Buttons, interactive cards, input wrappers, and imagery utilize pristine right angles, contrasting with circular micro-dots (such as active navigation pips) used strictly as functional status indicators.

## Components

### Buttons & Action Links
- **Primary Text Action:** Minimalist inline lockup featuring uppercase `label-caps` text followed by a horizontal arrow glyph (`→`). On hover, the arrow translates right (`transform: translateX(6px)`) and shifts from white to `#E6C84A`.
- **Solid Button:** Rectangular sharp border, solid `#E6C84A` fill, dark `#050606` text, zero radius. Hover transitions to `filter: brightness(1.1)`.
- **Ghost/Outline Button:** Hairline border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)` with transparent fill and `#F5F5F5` typography, transitioning on hover to gold border and gold text.

### Navigation Links
- Horizontal uppercase typography with `letter-spacing: 0.18em`.
- The active link features a miniature solid gold circle (`4px x 4px`) positioned directly underneath with a `6px` vertical offset.

### Project & Portfolio Cards
- Monolithic rectangular framing with a 1px border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`).
- Imagery utilizes low-saturation grading with smooth transition to natural color and a subtle scale shift (`1.02x`) upon cursor focus.
- Project details are pinned to the lower edge, divided by a horizontal hairline border.

### Chips & Meta Badges
- Sharp-edged pill alternatives: 1px border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`), transparent interior, `label-caps` font sizing, padded with `0.25rem 0.75rem`.

### Form Elements & Inputs
- Minimalist underline or boxed input fields with `#0B0C0C` fill, bottom-only border in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`. Focus states illuminate the border in `#E6C84A` without harsh focus rings.

### Social Rails & Floating Rails
- Vertical left-hand utility rail featuring mono-line social iconography (`20px`) with 50% opacity neutral tint, brightening to 100% white on hover.
