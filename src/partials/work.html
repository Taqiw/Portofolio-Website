Background

This project analyzes the decline in profitability of the Corporate segment across the Central US region throughout 2014. The objective was to diagnose the drivers behind that decline and recommend a strategy capable of increasing segment profitability by 15% in 2015.

Key Findings

  1. Technology — led by the Copiers and Phones subcategories — together with Furniture (Chairs and Tables) were the largest contributors to total profit.
  2. The state of Indiana recorded the highest total profit of all administrative regions, at $9,627.96.
  3. Shipping cost showed only a weak positive correlation with profit, and offering discounts did not consistently translate into higher sales.

Business Recommendations

  1. 01 Focus digital marketing spend on the best-performing product lines.
  2. 02 Optimize territories identified as having the highest profit potential.
  3. 03 Improve logistics efficiency to reduce the cost burden on thin-margin orders.
  4. 04 Replace the blanket discount strategy with a product bundling system.
